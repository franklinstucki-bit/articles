**Quick answer:** Crashes on San Juan, Orcas and Lopez islands often involve narrow two-lane roads, visitors unfamiliar with them, rented mopeds and cars, deer, and ferry-unloading traffic. The same Washington laws apply as on the mainland, but island logistics make early evidence and early medical records especially important.

The ferry horn sounds off Friday Harbor. Ramps drop. A line of cars rolls up Spring Street at once, every driver in a hurry to be somewhere, half of them on the island for the first time.

An hour later the roads are quiet again. Fog sits in the hollows. A deer stands at the edge of Roche Harbor Road as if it owns it.

## Why are island crashes different?

The islands have their own rhythm, and their own hazards:

- Narrow roads with little or no shoulder, tight curves and blind crests
- Bicycles, walkers and farm vehicles sharing the same lane
- Rented mopeds, scooters and cars driven by visitors
- Black-tailed deer, especially at dusk and after dark
- Bursts of traffic when a ferry unloads in Friday Harbor, Orcas or Lopez

Washington's rules still apply. [RCW 46.61.400](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=46.61.400) calls for reduced speed on narrow or winding roads, at hill crests and curves, and when pedestrians or conditions create special hazards. Island roads check every one of those boxes.

## What about ferry lines and terminals?

The [Washington State Ferries](https://wsdot.wa.gov/travel/washington-state-ferries) route from Anacortes serves Lopez, Shaw, Orcas and Friday Harbor. Low-speed collisions happen in holding lanes, on ramps, and on the roads just beyond the terminals.

For a damage-only collision, [RCW 46.52.020](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=46.52.020) directs drivers to move to a safe location and exchange names, addresses, insurance and license information. If anyone is hurt, stop, call 911, and give reasonable assistance.

If ferry staff or a ferry-system vehicle may be involved, claims against the state follow the pre-suit process in [RCW 4.92.110](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=4.92.110).

## Is a moped or scooter rental covered like a car?

Not always. Washington's mandatory liability insurance chapter, [RCW 46.30.020](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=46.30.020), lists mopeds among its exceptions. The [Insurance Commissioner](https://www.insurance.wa.gov/insurance-resources/auto-insurance/how-auto-insurance-works/personal-injury-protection-pip) also lists mopeds among standard PIP exclusions.

That means coverage after a moped crash can depend on the rental agreement, the rider's own policies, and any driver who hit them. Ask the rental company for its insurance information in writing.

## What if a visitor hit me, or I am the visitor?

Visitors leave on the next boat. Get their information before they do: name, phone, address, insurer, policy number, plate and driver's license. Photograph the documents if they agree.

If you are visiting and were hurt, do not wait until you are home in another state to start the claim. Island witnesses and video are easier to find this week than next month.

## What should I do after an island crash?

1. Call 911. Island responders know the roads.
2. Get medical care on the island before you board a ferry, and keep the records.
3. Photograph the road, the shoulder, signs and sight lines.
4. Ask nearby businesses and homes about cameras.
5. Note the ferry sailing you came off, if it matters to timing.
6. Notify your insurer and ask about PIP and UIM.

## Local specifics

Friday Harbor's downtown streets, Roche Harbor Road, Beaverton Valley Road, and the winding roads up Orcas toward Eastsound all have their own patterns. Summer brings visitors and rental fleets. Winter brings early dark, rain and fog. Follow-up care may mean a ferry ride to Anacortes and a drive to Mount Vernon or Bellingham. Keep track of that travel.

## FAQ

### Does it matter that the crash happened on an island?

The law is the same. The practical challenges, like witnesses leaving and limited local care, are what change.

### What if a county vehicle was involved?

Claims against local government have a required pre-suit claim and waiting period under [RCW 4.96.020](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=4.96.020).

### Can I handle the claim after I go home?

Yes, but starting early protects evidence. Many steps can be done remotely.
